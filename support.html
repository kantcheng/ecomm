Support

Shipping · Returns · Warranty · FAQ

Shipping & Delivery

  • Orders ship from our warehouse in the Netherlands within 1–2 business days.
  • Standard EU delivery: 2–4 business days (DPD / DHL), tracking included.
  • Free shipping on orders over €50; flat €4.90 below that.
  • We ship to all EU member states. Prices include VAT (OSS) — no customs fees inside the EU.
  • Batteries ship under UN38.3 compliant packaging; power banks must travel in carry-on luggage when flying.

Returns & Refunds

  • 30-day free returns on everything — prepaid return label included in every parcel.
  • Under EU consumer law you additionally have a 14-day right of withdrawal without giving a reason.
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days after we receive the return.
  • Start a return: email support@voltaro.eu with your order number.

Warranty

Every Voltaro product carries an 18-month warranty covering manufacturing defects, on top of the statutory 2-year conformity guarantee under EU Directive 2019/771. Register nothing — just keep your order confirmation. Defective units are replaced, not repaired.

FAQ

  • Can I pay in installments? Yes — Klarna offers pay-later and 3-installment plans at checkout.
  • Do the chargers work with iPhone / Samsung / MacBook? Yes, all USB-C PD products are universal. The MagSnap wireless charger supports MagSafe-compatible phones.
  • Are power banks flight-safe? Yes — the VoltCore 20K is within the 100 Wh airline limit, but carry it in hand luggage.
  • Which plug standard do you ship? EU Schuko (Type F) by default; UK and CH adapters available on request.
  • Where is my order? Check the tracking link in your shipping email, or write to us — we answer within 24 h.
