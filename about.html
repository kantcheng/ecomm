About Voltaro

Live charged. Anywhere in Europe.

Our story

Voltaro was founded by a small team of engineers and frequent travellers who were tired of dead batteries on the 6 a.m. train from Rotterdam to Berlin. We build charging and audio gear that is engineered for European life — Schuko plugs, EU-certified safety, multilingual support and a warehouse in the middle of it all.

What makes us different

  • EU-first: CE, RoHS and WEEE compliant. Prices include VAT under the One-Stop-Shop (OSS) scheme — no surprise customs fees.
  • Built to last: every product carries an 18-month warranty and passes 35,000-bend and temperature-stress testing before launch.
  • Real support: humans in Amsterdam answering in English, German and French within 24 hours.
  • Responsible: plastic-free packaging, and we recycle e-waste free of charge under EU WEEE rules.
