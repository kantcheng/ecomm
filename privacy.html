Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This template is provided for demonstration. Adapt it to your actual data processing and have it reviewed legally before going live (GDPR Arts. 13/14).

1. Who we are (controller)

Voltaro B.V., Keizersgracht 123, 1015 CJ Amsterdam, The Netherlands. Contact for privacy matters: privacy@voltaro.eu.

2. What data we collect and why

  • Order data (name, address, email, items purchased) — to fulfil your contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Payment data — processed by our PCI-DSS certified payment providers; we never store full card numbers.
  • Cart contents — stored locally in your browser (localStorage) so your cart survives page reloads.
  • Newsletter email — only with your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)); unsubscribe any time via the footer link.
  • Analytics data — anonymised traffic statistics, only after you accept analytics cookies.

3. Cookies

  • Essential: consent record, cart storage. Always active — required for the shop to function.
  • Analytics: anonymous usage statistics. Active only with your consent via the cookie banner.
  • You can change or withdraw consent any time by clearing site data in your browser.

4. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. Email privacy@voltaro.eu and we will respond within 30 days. You may also lodge a complaint with the Dutch DPA (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

5. Data location & retention

All data is hosted on servers inside the EU. Order records are kept for 7 years (Dutch tax law); analytics for 14 months maximum.

6. Recipients

We share the minimum necessary data with: payment providers (PayPal, Klarna, Stripe), carriers (DPD, DHL) and our email platform — all under EU-standard contractual clauses or EU-based processing.
